🚀 Getting Started

📋 First Time Setup

▼

Follow these steps to get started with Aura Universe:

  1. Invite the bot to your server using our setup
  2. Use !bothelp to see all commands
  3. Start your journey by following guide(if your server have one)

📖 Basic Commands

▼
  • !aura - Check your aura balance
  • !profile - View your profile
  • !leaderboard - View top players
  • !inventory -view your inventory items

💰 Economy & Currency

⛏️ Mining System

▼

Mine ores to earn aura and level up!

  • !mine - Start mining
  • !companyshop <name> - Buy and pgrade your pickaxe
  • !sell <"name"< <quantity< - Sell your ores

🎣 Fishing System

▼
  • !fish - Go fishing
  • !companyshop <name> - Get better rods
  • !sell <"name"< <quantity< - Sell your catch

🏖️ Beachcombing

▼
  • !beachcomb - Search the beach
  • !beach_inventory - View your finds

🎮 Mini Games

🏃‍♂️ Escape Rooms

▼

Work together to solve puzzles and escape!

  • !escape lobby - Create/join a lobby
  • !escape themes - See available themes
  • !escape start - Start the game
  • !escape answer <answer> - Solve puzzles

Themes available: 🏚️ Haunted House, 🚀 Space Station, 🪦 Egypt Tomb, 🧪 Mad Scientist

🎭 Party Games

▼
  • !mg bomb - Number Bomb (elimination game)
  • !mg count - Counting Up (collision game)
  • !mg wyr - Would You Rather
  • !emojigame - Guess the emoji sentence
  • !memescenario - Meme challenge

🐺 Werewolf Game

▼

Social deduction game for 5-15 players!

  • !ww join - Join the game
  • !ww start - Start the game
  • !ww vote - Vote to eliminate

🎰 Casino & Betting

▼
  • !casino - Open casino menu
  • !slots <bet> - Play slots
  • !wheel <bet> - Spin the wheel
  • !roulette <bet> - Play roulette
  • !bet @user <amount> - Challenge someone

🏢 Companies

🏭 Creating a Company

▼
  • !createcompany - Start your own company
  • !company - View your company profile
  • !company_edit - Edit company details

📦 Selling Items

▼
  • !listsale "item" <price> <quantity> - List items
  • !companyshop <company> - Browse shops
  • !mysales - View your listings
  • !removesale <id> - Remove listing

Example: !listsale "Steel Rod" 500 5

🏎️ Car Racing

🏁 Getting Started with Racing

▼
  • !setupracingteam - Create your racing team
  • !catalog - View available cars
  • !buycar <name> - Purchase a car
  • !dealership - See daily deals

🏆 Racing & Betting

▼
  • !createrace - Host a race

  • !betonrace - Bet on winners
  • !raceresults - View results
